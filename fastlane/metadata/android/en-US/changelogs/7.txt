Version 1.5:
- Opens straight into the AR view. The main menu is gone.
- One slim bottom row for one-handed use, with a left-handed option in Settings.
- Points now lock on plain walls.
- Simpler reticle and a single cyan line; steadier live reading shown in ft in or m cm.
- AR pauses itself after a minute idle to save battery. Tap to continue.
- Simpler Settings, History and About. Handy formulas and the Clear button are removed.
